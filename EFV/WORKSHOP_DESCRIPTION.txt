[h1]Volunteer & Expeditionary Forces[/h1]
[i]Version 0.6.0-dev (development build). Requires Gathering Storm.[/i]

Lend your combat units to the civs and city-states that fight your wars, and hand captured cities to your partners.

[h2]What you can do[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Expeditionary Force:[/b] send a land or naval combat unit to an ally, teammate or declared friend who shares one of your wars. They control it for 20 turns, then it comes home.
[*][b]Volunteers:[/b] send units to an ally, a teammate, or a declared friend who gives you open borders. You keep control and pay the upkeep, with no time limit. You can recall them after 10 turns.
[*][b]City-State Expeditionary:[/b] lend a unit for 10 turns to a city-state you have met that fights one of your enemies.
[*][b]Entrust:[/b] give a city you just captured to an ally, teammate or declared friend who was at war with its former owner (any partner for a Free City). Works for capitals and city-state cities too. The capture screen gets an Entrust button that lists who can take the city, or says why nobody can.
[/list]

Units leave the map for 1 to 4 turns, depending on the distance between the two cities (up to 10, 20, 35 or more tiles on a Standard map, scaled by map size). They keep their promotions and any upgrade the host gives them. Their level may reset on arrival.

Fees are a share of the unit's gold price. Expeditionary and City-State units are free for a 1-turn trip, then 10, 20 or 30%. Volunteers cost 20, 30, 40 or 50%. You also pay normal upkeep while a unit travels. The host does not need the tech for a unit it receives.

[h2]Bring them home in time[/h2]
When its service ends, a lent unit has to stand on its host's land or yours. If it doesn't, it gets 5 turns of grace and then [b]mutinies[/b]: 20 damage per turn and no healing until it gets back or dies.

Volunteers also lapse when the partnership or the shared war ends. They never come home on their own. On your land or the partner's their countdown waits until you recall them, and the lapse ends if the partnership comes back.

[h2]Interface[/h2]
[list]
[*]Send and Recall buttons in the unit panel. The tooltips list every reason a unit can't go.
[*]A destination list with distance, travel time, fee and length of service for every partner city.
[*]EXP / VOL / CS badges on unit flags.
[*]A VEF tracker on the launch bar with every unit you sent or received, its state, turns left and destination. Click a row to jump to the unit.
[*]A red banner and repeated notifications while a unit has to get back.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Requires Gathering Storm.
[*]VEF replaces the unit flag script (UnitFlagManager) with a thin wrapper for its badges. Gift It To Me and other mods that replace the same script can win over it. Then the badges disappear, but everything else still works.
[*]CQUI should work but has not been tested.
[*]Tested in single player only. Multiplayer should work but has not been tested.
[*]Saved with your game. Don't remove it in the middle of a game.
[/list]

[h2]Known limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*]The new owner of an entrusted city takes no extra penalty and gets no loyalty help, so a city it can't hold may flip. A matching grievance penalty is planned but needs a game function that doesn't exist yet.
[*]A unit whose owner has none of its strategic resource does not heal (a game rule). This also applies to lent units. VEF warns you in the destination list, the tracker and the unit flag.
[*]If a host is eliminated, its lent units come home as they were when its last city fell. A unit killed in that fight is lost.
[*]Units keep their promotions, but their level may reset when they reappear.
[*]A host can merge a lent unit into a Corps or Army. An absorbed unit is lost to you. A surviving unit comes home alone.
[*]The AI never sends units, but it uses the ones it receives.
[/list]
